ABOUT PROBETALENT

A focused partner for specialist recruitment.

ProbeTalent provides recruitment and talent acquisition services, from the placement of individual specialists to the development and delivery of comprehensive recruitment programmes tailored to client business objectives.

We serve organisations across Consumer Products & Retail, Pharmaceuticals & Life Sciences, and Industrial Products, offering specialised talent solutions aligned with industry requirements and transformation priorities.

We operate on a B2B basis, connecting organisations with the expertise required to deliver business-critical initiatives.

Our revenue comes from recruitment fees, placement commissions and programme delivery services. Related services may include talent consulting, skills assessment, workforce planning advisory and staffing solutions, together with directly related activities that support these services.

    SWISS DATA PROTECTION

    Candidate Privacy Notice

    Draft for review before launch. This website currently prepares an email; it does not transmit or store CV files. This notice must be completed with ProbeTalent’s verified legal details, actual systems, recipients and retention schedule before live collection.

    1. Controller and contact

    The intended controller is ProbeTalent (full registered company name, postal address and privacy contact to be confirmed before launch). Please do not send sensitive information until a verified contact channel is published.

    2. Information and purposes

    When a live application service is introduced, ProbeTalent may process details you provide, such as your name, contact information, location, professional experience, job preferences and CV, to respond to your enquiry, assess relevant roles and communicate about recruitment. We will limit collection to what is necessary.

    3. Recipients and international disclosures

    Application data may be shared with relevant prospective employers and contracted technology providers only where appropriate for the stated purpose. Any transfers outside Switzerland must be assessed under Articles 16–17 FADP and disclosed with the destination country and applicable safeguards before they occur.

    4. Retention and security

    Data should be retained only for as long as necessary for the specified recruitment purpose or applicable legal requirements, with a documented deletion schedule and appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. The precise retention periods and hosting arrangements are pending confirmation.

    5. Your rights and choices

    Subject to Swiss law, you may request information about your data, correction of inaccurate data and deletion where applicable. You can also withdraw optional consent for future job alerts. A verified privacy contact will be provided before the live service launches.
